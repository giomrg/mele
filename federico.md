## How to play Kabo
Kabo (also spelled Cabo or Cambio) is a card game based on memory, strategy, and a little bit of bluffing.

The goal is to finish with the lowest total value of cards.

Here's how the game works:
1. Setup: Each player receives 4 face-down cards and can secretly look at 2 of them at the beginning.
2. Your turn: You can draw a card from the deck or take the top card from the discard pile, usually replacing one of your own cards.
3. Special actions: Depending on the version, certain cards allow you to peek at your own cards, look at an opponent's cards, or swap cards with another player.
4. Memory: You need to remember your cards' values because you generally cannot look at them whenever you want.
5. Calling Kabo: When you think you have the lowest total, you call "Kabo!" The other players get one final turn before everyone reveals their cards.
